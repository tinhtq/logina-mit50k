WHY LOGINA

See more of what students understand.

An answer can leave the reasoning behind it unclear. LOGINA is being built to make that reasoning easier to practice, explain and understand.

Students explain their thinking

Practice putting ideas into words, using evidence and considering different perspectives.

Teachers see where to help

See patterns in students’ explanations, identify gaps in understanding and decide what to teach next.

More room for discussion

Bring different ideas into the conversation and connect them to the learning goal.

A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Questions about LOGINA?

A little more context before we start a conversation.

What stage is LOGINA at?

LOGINA is in early product development. We are seeking educators and schools to explore classroom use. Findings from future pilots will guide further development.

Which learners is LOGINA for?

We are exploring suitable subjects and age groups for initial pilots. Share your teaching context when you register, and we will discuss fit before a demo or pilot.

What role does AI play?

AI supports learning within goals set by the teacher. Students develop and explain their own ideas. A future demo will offer a closer look at the experience.

What can I expect from a demo?

Register through the Google Form to receive a suitable link and instructions by email. What you can explore will depend on the current product and your area of interest.

Does registration guarantee a school pilot?

Registration is free. The email provides a suitable link and instructions. A school pilot or partnership needs a separate discussion and is subject to availability.
